Before Embeddings

Problem:

Memory retrieval needs meaning matching.

Word matching is insufficient.

---

Researchers asked:

What is meaning mathematically?

---

Key Insight:

Maybe meanings can be represented like locations.

Similar meanings:

Close together.

Different meanings:

Far apart.

---

Analogy:

Cities have coordinates.

Meanings could also have coordinates.

---

If:

live and reside

are close in meaning-space,

then:

Query:
"Where does Alice live?"

Memory:
"Alice resides in Paris."

can match successfully.

---

Core Idea:

Represent words, sentences, memories, and queries as points in a semantic space.

Then retrieval becomes:

Find nearest meanings

instead of

Find matching words.

---

This conceptual idea leads directly to embeddings.
